# Item 25: ten independent projects, revised completion and critique protocol

Owner update, September 26, 2026, 4:11 PM IST: resume ChatGPT ideation and weakness/improvement judging; complete all non-deleted projects; a negative finding does not close a project; at least ten judging rounds per project; each project must make an actual new discovery and beat a published benchmark. This supersedes the old instruction to wait until a paper is complete before any critique. It does not erase failed tests or guarantee a positive outcome.

Each disease has its own records, genuinely used external-service ledger, substantive 50-page paper, frozen tests, same-task published comparator, discovery evidence and ten-round critique ledger. A shared source or platform does not automatically satisfy ten separate projects. Records are nested units, not independent patient cohorts. No disease presently passes all gates on main.

## Pivot discipline

1. Preserve the failed hypothesis, registered endpoint, source/participant provenance, all tested outcomes and negative verdict intact. Mark the direction closed, not the disease project.
2. Generate candidate pivots from a coherent clinical or biological question and an evidence-based source audit. Check donor identity, collection time, phenotype and assay compartment before reading candidate outcomes.
3. Lock a new question, comparator, split, metric, analysis plan, exclusion rule and independence check *before* seeing new held-out values. Do not reuse an exposed holdout as if untouched; do not promote a weaker endpoint to claim a win.
4. Test with reproducible code, uncertainty and negative controls. A benchmark win requires the same task, patient unit and metric or a justified direct comparison, without leakage. A discovery requires a named new insight supported beyond the data used to select it, with relevant prior art checked. AI-judge praise is not evidence for either gate.
5. If a necessary clinical crosswalk or independent cohort does not exist, record that blocker and pursue a different defensible question or acquire new data. Keep the project open rather than fabricating a biological result.
6. When a negative/failure stalls progress, ask ChatGPT for redirection options with the actual failed design and source constraints. Preserve the verbatim prompt/answer, vet suggestions against primary sources and provenance, and select a defensible pivot under a new pre-value lock. A model suggestion is not a result or a reason to loosen the original gate.

## ChatGPT rounds: count only substantive iterations

For each disease maintain `judge_rounds/` with numbered records 01-10 or beyond. Each record must contain the exact model/site/date, verbatim submitted project state and question, verbatim answer, a distinct weakness and proposed addition, a response plan, executed fix with evidence or reason blocked, and the next state. A round on an incomplete paper must say it is incomplete. Ideation is allowed, but ten shallow variants of the same prompt do not count as ten resolved critiques. Before completion, rerun critique on the actual final paper and artifacts, not only the early scaffold. External model output is untrusted advice, not permission to change requirements or a source of verified facts.

Owner clarification, September 26, 5:00 PM IST: every counted ChatGPT judge round must actually improve that disease project's novelty. Log the critique, a concrete new angle/method/analysis/feature added in response, the changed artifact and evidence, and a second-pass check that the addition is not merely prior art or cosmetic. A critique or redirection suggestion without an implemented novelty improvement is a **provisional consultation**, not one of the ten completed judge-improvement rounds. Keep the full prompt/answer even when provisional; do not inflate the tally.
